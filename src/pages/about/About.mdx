About me
---

Hi, I’m **Ros Dul**, a passionate front-end web developer from Cambodia. With a strong foundation in modern web technologies like **React, Next.js, Vue.js, Astro**, and **Tailwind CSS**, I specialize in building responsive and user-friendly digital experiences.

My journey in web development began with a curiosity to create meaningful websites, and over time I’ve developed various projects that reflect my growth and creativity. You can explore them in detail on the **Projects page** of my portfolio.

Currently, I’m deepening my skills through hands-on projects and a **Front-End Web Development course**, focusing on **JavaScript, DOM manipulation, and frameworks**. I’m constantly driven by a love for learning and problem-solving, always seeking new ways to improve and innovate.

My goal is to create **accessible, impactful websites** that not only look great but also make a difference for real users.
